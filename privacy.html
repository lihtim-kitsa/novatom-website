Privacy policy

This policy explains how NovAtom Labs handles information when you visit this website or send a request through it.

What you share

The Request access form asks for your name, email address, and a description of what you want to solve. You can also include your organisation. Please leave out sensitive personal information and confidential research details.

How we use your request

NovAtom Labs uses your submission to review your enquiry, reply by email, and discuss a possible pilot or other follow-up you request.

Services that handle information

Submissions go to Formspree, which processes them and makes them available to NovAtom Labs. Formspree may also receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, and may process information in the United States and other countries where it operates. Formspree privacy policy

Vercel hosts this deployment and receives the network information needed to serve pages, such as your IP address. Vercel privacy notice

Questions and requests

To ask about retention, request access to or correction or deletion of your submission, or ask us to stop following up, use the contact form and start your message with “Privacy request.” Use the email address from your original submission if you have access to it.
