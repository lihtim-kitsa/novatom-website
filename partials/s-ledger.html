Language models plan. Simulators and instruments measure.

Every quantitative result comes from a deterministic tool: a simulator, an instrument or a parser. A language model never produces a number. A validator then decides whether each result counts.

Results are stored with their method, unit, uncertainty, validity range and a trust tier, so the system knows how much weight each one deserves.
